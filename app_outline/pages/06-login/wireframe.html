User Story

As a returning reader, I want to log in with my email and password, so that I can get back to my library and logs.

Key Elements

  • Navigation bar (guest)
  • Email field
  • Password field
  • Error message area
  • Log In button (shows "Signing in…")
  • Link to Sign Up
